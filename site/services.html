FAQ

Useful details before you inquire.

Are these fixed prices?

No. They are starting prices. Final pricing depends on the actual page count, content readiness, integrations, custom functionality, and timeline.

Can I request something outside these packages?

Yes. Redesigns, responsive cleanup, accessibility work, support, and other focused website improvements can be scoped separately.

What does monthly maintenance cover?

Maintenance is $100/month and is available only for websites I built. It includes 3 small fixes and 1 small feature each month, with unused fixes and features rolling over up to 6 each.

What counts as a small feature?

A modest addition such as a new section, form, gallery, FAQ, simple page, or small e-commerce improvement. Larger custom functionality is quoted separately.

Who is the $50 Community Starter for?

It is a limited-scope option for select woman-owned, Black-owned, or animal-centered small businesses. Projects are selected based on fit, need, availability, and whether the requested scope can stay intentionally small.

Do you guarantee SEO rankings or conversion results?

No. I build strong SEO/accessibility fundamentals and conversion-minded structure, but I do not promise search rankings, revenue, or fabricated performance results.
