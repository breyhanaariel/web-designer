Before / After · Small-Business Redesign

🐾 Poppy & Paws

A self-directed redesign study showing how a cluttered local-service website can become easier to understand, easier to trust, easier to find, and easier to contact—without inventing client results.

Concept redesign · No real client or fabricated metrics

Before & After

From “there’s a website” to “I know what to do next.”

The fictional before state combines common small-business problems: buried services, competing calls to action, weak mobile hierarchy, vague local information, and inconsistent accessibility. The after state reorganizes the experience around the decisions a prospective grooming client actually needs to make.

Business Outcomes

Every redesign decision should earn its place.

Better inquiriesAsk for pet type, service, timing, and contact details up front so the business can respond with less back-and-forth.
Faster service comparisonReplace dense paragraphs with package cards that explain starting prices and what is included.
Stronger local clarityUse clear service-area language, useful FAQs, and descriptive headings that help people and search engines understand the offer.
Business goalRedesign decisionIntended impact
Generate more qualified inquiriesPrimary booking CTA + structured inquiry detailsReduce back-and-forth and improve lead quality
Make services easier to comparePackage cards with starting prices and inclusionsHelp visitors self-select a relevant service faster
Improve discoverabilityDescriptive service/local headings + useful FAQsCreate clearer search and customer context
Build trustProcess, care standards, policies, and space for future genuine reviewsReduce uncertainty without fake social proof
Improve accessibilityContrast, semantic structure, labels, focus states, larger controlsMake the site usable by more visitors across devices
Measurement note: On a real redesign I would compare inquiry completion, qualified lead volume, click-to-call/book behavior, mobile abandonment, relevant organic landing traffic, and accessibility issues before vs. after. No result is fabricated here.

SEO + Accessibility Fundamentals

Foundational work, not decorative checkboxes.

🔎 Search fundamentalsDescriptive titles and headings, useful local/service copy, FAQ content, clear business details, and human-readable navigation.
♿ Accessibility fundamentalsSemantic landmarks, labeled forms, visible keyboard focus, contrast-conscious styling, large touch targets, and logical mobile reading order.
