Before / After Redesign

A redesign should make the business easier to understand—and easier to hire.

🐾 Poppy & Paws is a clearly labeled fictional pet-grooming redesign study. It demonstrates how I would turn common small-business website problems into clearer services, stronger local/search context, better accessibility, and a simpler inquiry path without inventing client results.

Poppy & Paws

From cluttered service site to clear booking journey.

  • Before: buried services, competing CTAs, weak mobile hierarchy, vague local information, generic visual identity.
  • After: one primary booking path, scannable packages, starting-price context, useful FAQs, service-area clarity, and accessibility fundamentals.
  • Intended outcome: fewer questions before contact, better-qualified inquiries, stronger trust, and a more understandable local-service offer.
