## Accessibility & responsive review

Implemented and checked at code/live-route level:
- semantic page landmarks and heading structures
- real buttons for state-changing controls
- form labels on demo/booking/inquiry flows
- ARIA state on tab/filter controls where used
- native keyboard focus retained
- reduced-motion CSS support
- narrow-screen responsive breakpoints
- no critical information dependent on hover alone
- concept forms clearly distinguish demo behavior where appropriate

The sites target WCAG 2.2-informed AA design. A formal third-party conformance certification is not claimed.

## Inquiry / freelance conversion review

The client-facing portfolio now supports a straightforward freelance path:

1. Prospect sees services and starting prices.
2. Prospect reviews live work and the before/after redesign study.
3. Prospect completes a short inquiry form with project type, budget, timeline, pages, and goals.
4. The form submits through FormSubmit's AJAX form endpoint; direct email remains available as a fallback.

**Activation note:** FormSubmit requires the receiving email address to confirm the form the first time it is used. Until that one-time confirmation is completed, the portfolio should continue to present direct email as a backup contact path.
